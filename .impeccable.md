## Design Context

### Users
Sigge is a public copy workshop for people who want to turn their own facts, audience and offer into useful writing instructions. First-time visitors should not need to know copy frameworks or MCP. The owner wants Swedish that stands on its own, and an equally usable English service.

### Brand Personality
Clear, warm, direct. Help people keep their own voice and make informed choices. Explain the handoff to their AI chat without suggesting that Sigge has already generated or reviewed their text.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm editorial paper (#f7f4ee), ink type and blue actions. Fraunces for display and the wordmark, Inter for interface text, both self-hosted in public/fonts (OFL). Shared tokens and components live in public/site.css; header and footer are partials in client/pages. Explainer films are DOM scenes on a deep blue stage (client/film.js): each chapter's progress bar is the clock, base styles are the final frame, and reduced motion shows static frames. Exported MP4s of the films live in public/media and are rendered frame by frame from the same scenes.

### Design Principles
- Start with task, reader and facts; disclose optional controls progressively.
- Make the next action explicit and distinguish examples from the user's own work.
- Keep interface language separate from output language; preserve work when switching.
- Give both languages complete navigation, examples, search and instructions.
- Preserve facts, terms, provenance and the user's choice about browser storage.
- Motion explains a step or confirms an action; it is never decoration alone, can always be paused and respects reduced motion.

# Copy chief: 25 checks

Apply only relevant checks to the requested scope. These are diagnostic prompts, not a numeric quality score. Mark an item as supported, needs work, unknown or not applicable when a detailed review is requested. Cite the exact passage for material issues, explain the problem and propose a fix grounded in available facts. If evidence is missing, say what would verify the claim. Finish with the copy and the unresolved blockers rather than a decorative checklist.

## Strategy and audience
1. The intended reader and their situation are recognizable.
2. The asset has a clear job and a suitable next action.
3. The central promise is understandable without insider knowledge.
4. The offer is described accurately, including relevant limits and costs.
5. The angle addresses a real buyer need rather than an assumed one.
6. The positioning gives a defensible reason to choose the offer when differentiation matters.
7. Objections and alternatives are addressed where necessary.

## Argument and evidence
8. The reader can connect a feature or mechanism to a meaningful benefit.
9. Promises match the evidence in scope, dates, population and magnitude.
10. Numbers, timelines and comparisons have traceable sources.
11. Quotes, testimonials and examples are genuine and approved for the intended use.
12. Causation is not inferred from coincidence, correlation or a single anecdote.
13. Scarcity, guarantees, pricing and terms are real and current.
14. Unsupported or consequential claims are removed, qualified or flagged for verification.
15. Proof appears near the claim where it will help the reader assess it.

## Craft and action
16. The opening earns attention without misleading the reader.
17. The wording is specific when facts support specificity; clarity beats forced novelty.
18. The draft preserves the author's actual voice and chosen argument.
19. Customer quotations are sourced; paraphrases are not presented as verbatim speech.
20. Sentences are understandable; terminology is defined for the audience.
21. Each section contributes to the argument without redundant filler.
22. Tone, vocabulary and rhythm fit the channel and market.
23. The action describes a genuine next step and matches the destination.
24. The text avoids unsupported pressure or claims of performance.
25. The deliverable respects requested format and length; remaining unknowns are visible.

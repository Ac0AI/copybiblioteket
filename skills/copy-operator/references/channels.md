# Channel guidance

Use only the parts relevant to the requested asset. Match existing brand and user instructions over default structure.

## Landing and product pages
Choose a clear primary action. Make the offer and audience understandable near the top; develop the argument using relevant benefits, evidence, objections, terms and the next step. Proof should support the claim where it appears. Include only relevant sections. Keep the action consistent with what happens on click.

## Ads
Connect the hook to the actual offer and destination. When options are requested, vary the underlying argument, not merely synonyms; note audience, angle and the assumption each tests. Respect supplied platform limits and verify current rules when needed. Do not infer success from ad visibility, longevity, competitor examples or engagement alone. Research examples for patterns, not for copying claims or creative.

## Emails
Fit the existing relationship and message purpose. Provide subject and body when relevant, and a clear reason for the recipient to act or reply. Use verified personalization; never imply a conversation or relationship that did not occur. A sequence should have a distinct purpose per message and an appropriate cadence. Sending requires explicit authorization and the applicable sender verification rules.

## Experiments
Give the baseline, changed message, audience, action, metric and decision condition when enough information is available. If volumes or tracking are unknown, say so. A proposed variation is a hypothesis until observed results support a conclusion; do not equate clicks with sales.
